# Standard Operating Procedure: Copper Nanoparticle (NanoCu) Synthesis

## Document Information
- **SOP ID**: NanoCu-SOP-001
- **Version**: 1.0
- **Date**: 2024
- **Scale**: Pilot-scale (10-100 g batch)
- **Classification**: Materials Synthesis - Nanoparticles

## 1. Purpose
This SOP describes the procedure for synthesizing copper nanoparticles (NanoCu) via thermal decomposition in a high-boiling point solvent system with surfactant stabilization.

## 2. Scope
This procedure applies to pilot-scale production of copper nanoparticles for materials science applications.

## 3. Safety PrecautIONS
- **PPE Required**: Safety goggles, nitrile gloves, lab coat, face shield
- **Hazards**: Hot oil bath (110°C), organic solvents, metal precursors
- **Emergency**: Fire extinguisher (Class B/C) nearby, eyewash station accessible
- **Ventilation**: Fume hood required for all steps

## 4. Materials and Equipment

### 4.1 Chemicals
| Item | Specification | Quantity (per 10g batch) | CAS Number |
|------|--------------|-------------------------|------------|
| Precursor A | Copper(II) acetate monohydrate, ≥98% | 5.0 g | 6046-93-1 |
| Solvent | Diphenyl ether or 1-octadecene | 100 mL | 101-84-8 / 112-88-9 |
| Surfactant | Oleylamine, technical grade | 20 mL | 112-90-3 |
| Quenching Agent | Ethanol, anhydrous | 500 mL | 64-17-5 |

### 4.2 Equipment
- Three-neck round-bottom flask (500 mL)
- Magnetic stirrer with hot plate
- Oil bath with temperature control (±2°C)
- Reflux condenser
- Nitrogen/vacuum manifold
- Schlenk line or inert atmosphere glovebox
- Centrifuge (≥4000 rpm capacity)
- Ultrasonic bath

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Preparation
1. **System Setup**: Assemble the three-neck flask with reflux condenser and nitrogen inlet
2. **Inert Atmosphere**: Purge the system with nitrogen for 15 minutes
3. **Solvent Addition**: Add 100 mL diphenyl ether to the flask
4. **Heating**: Begin heating oil bath to target temperature

### 5.2 Synthesis

#### Step 1: Temperature Stabilization
- **Target Temperature**: 110°C ± 2°C
- **Hold Time**: 15 minutes at target temperature
- **Stirring Rate**: 600 RPM
- **Atmosphere**: Maintain nitrogen flow (bubble rate: 1-2 bubbles/second)

#### Step 2: Precursor Addition
- **Rate**: Dropwise addition over 10-15 minutes
- **Observation**: Monitor for initial dissolution
- **Temperature**: Maintain 110°C throughout addition

#### Step 3: Surfactant Addition
- **Quantity**: 20 mL oleylamine
- **Method**: Rapid addition via syringe
- **Observation**: Solution should begin color change

#### Step 4: Reaction Hold
- **Duration**: 12-16 hours (overnight)
- **Temperature**: Maintain 110°C
- **Stirring**: Continue at 600 RPM
- **Critical Observation**: Color transition from blue-green to brown indicates nanoparticle formation

### 5.3 Workup and Quenching

#### Step 1: Cooling
- **Method**: Remove from heat, allow to cool to 60°C
- **Time**: Approximately 30-45 minutes
- **Atmosphere**: Maintain nitrogen blanket

#### Step 2: Precipitation
- Add 200 mL anhydrous ethanol to reaction mixture
- Stir for 15 minutes at room temperature
- Solution should turn cloudy indicating nanoparticle precipitation

#### Step 3: Isolation
- Transfer to centrifuge tubes
- Centrifuge at 4000 RPM for 15 minutes
- Decant supernatant
- Resuspend pellet in 50 mL ethanol
- Repeat wash cycle 2×

#### Step 4: Drying
- Final resuspension in minimal hexane or toluene
- Store under inert atmosphere
- Expected yield: 60-80% theoretical

## 6. Critical Process Parameters (CPP)

| Parameter | Target | Acceptable Range | Criticality |
|-----------|--------|------------------|-------------|
| Reaction Temperature | 110°C | 108-112°C | Critical |
| Precursor Addition Rate | Dropwise | 10-15 min | Critical |
| Reaction Time | 12-16 h | 10-18 h | High |
| Stirring Rate | 600 RPM | 500-700 RPM | Medium |
| Surfactant Ratio | 4:1 v/w | 3.5-4.5:1 | High |

## 7. Quality Control

### 7.1 In-Process Controls
- **Visual**: Color change monitoring (blue-green → brown)
- **Temperature**: Continuous logging required
- **Atmosphere**: Oxygen level < 10 ppm

### 7.2 Final Product Testing
| Test | Method | Specification |
|------|--------|---------------|
| Particle Size | TEM/DLS | 5-20 nm |
| Size Distribution | DLS | PDI < 0.2 |
| Crystal Phase | XRD | Cu (fcc) |
| Surface Chemistry | FTIR | Oleylamine coating |
| Purity | ICP-OES | >95% Cu |

## 8. Troubleshooting

| Issue | Possible Cause | Corrective Action |
|-------|---------------|-------------------|
| No color change | Insufficient temperature | Verify oil bath calibration |
| Large aggregates | Rapid precursor addition | Slow addition rate, increase surfactant |
| Low yield | Oxidation during workup | Improve inert atmosphere |
| Broad size distribution | Temperature fluctuation | Implement better temperature control |
