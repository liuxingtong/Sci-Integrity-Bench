## 1. PURPOSE

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) establishes the requirements and protocols for the compliant shipment of temperature-sensitive biologics specimens, ensuring maintenance of cold-chain integrity throughout the transportation process.

## 2. SCOPE

This SOP applies to all personnel involved in the packaging, handling, and shipment of biologics specimens requiring cold-chain maintenance, including:
- Clinical operations staff
- Packaging team members
- Logistics coordinators
- Quality assurance personnel

## 3. DEFINITIONS

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Cold Chain** | An unbroken temperature-controlled supply chain from collection to delivery |
| **Biologics** | Temperature-sensitive biological specimens including blood, tissue, and cellular products |
| **Primary Packaging** | Direct container holding the specimen |
| **Secondary Packaging** | Insulated container providing thermal protection |
| **Temperature Logger** | Electronic device recording temperature at defined intervals |
| **Qualified Packaging** | Packaging system validated to maintain required temperature range |

## 4. RESPONSIBILITIES

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| **Clinical Operations** | Overall cold-chain compliance and SOP maintenance |
| **Packaging Team** | Execution of secondary packaging protocols |
| **Logistics Coordinator** | Shipment scheduling and carrier coordination |
| **Quality Assurance** | Audit trail review and deviation investigation |
| **Vendor** | Temperature logger calibration and certification |

## 5. TEMPERATURE REQUIREMENTS

| Specimen Type | Temperature Range | Maximum Exposure Time |
|---------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Frozen Biologics | -20°C to -80°C | Per protocol specification |
| Refrigerated Biologics | 2°C to 8°C | 48 hours maximum |
| Ambient Biologics | 15°C to 25°C | Per protocol specification |

## 6. PACKAGING REQUIREMENTS

### 6.1 Primary Packaging
- Use leak-proof, shatter-resistant containers
- Ensure proper sealing and labeling
- Include absorbent material for liquid specimens

### 6.2 Secondary Packaging (To be finalized by Packaging Team)
- Insulated shipping container validated for required temperature range
- Phase-change materials (PCM) or gel packs conditioned to specification
- Temperature monitoring device placement
- Cushioning material to prevent movement

### 6.3 Outer Packaging
- Durable corrugated fiberboard
- "UN 3373 Biological Substance, Category B" labeling when applicable
- Orientation arrows and handling instructions
- Emergency contact information

## 7. TEMPERATURE MONITORING

### 7.1 Temperature Loggers
- Electronic data loggers must be placed with each shipment
- Loggers record temperature at minimum 15-minute intervals
- **Note:** Calibration certificates are maintained by the vendor; copies available upon request

### 7.2 Logger Placement
- Position logger in thermal center of secondary packaging
- Ensure logger does not contact phase-change materials directly
- Activate logger prior to packaging per manufacturer instructions

## 8. SHIPMENT PROCEDURES

### 8.1 Pre-Shipment Checklist
- [ ] Verify specimen integrity and labeling
- [ ] Confirm temperature requirements
- [ ] Check packaging system qualification status
- [ ] Program and activate temperature logger
- [ ] Complete chain of custody documentation
- [ ] Verify carrier cold-chain capabilities

### 8.2 Packaging Execution
1. Condition phase-change materials to specified temperature
2. Assemble secondary packaging system
3. Place primary containers with absorbent material
4. Position temperature logger in thermal center
5. Seal secondary packaging
6. Apply outer packaging and required labels
7. Complete shipping documentation

### 8.3 Documentation Requirements
- Chain of custody form
- Temperature monitoring device serial number
- Packaging configuration record
- Carrier tracking information
- Emergency contact card

## 9. RECEIPT AND VERIFICATION

### 9.1 Upon Receipt
1. Inspect outer packaging for damage or tampering
2. Verify temperature logger data
3. Check specimen integrity
4. Document receipt time and condition
5. Report deviations immediately to Quality Assurance

### 9.2 Temperature Data Review
- Download logger data within 24 hours of receipt
- Verify temperature remained within specification
- Archive data per record retention policy
- Investigate and document any excursions

## 10. AUDIT TRAIL

All cold-chain shipments must maintain complete documentation including:
- Shipment request and authorization
- Packaging and handling records
- Temperature monitoring data
- Chain of custody documentation
- Deviation reports (if applicable)

## 11. TRAINING REQUIREMENTS

Personnel involved in cold-chain shipment activities must complete training on:
- This SOP and related procedures
- Proper packaging techniques
- Temperature monitoring device operation
- Documentation requirements
- Emergency response procedures
