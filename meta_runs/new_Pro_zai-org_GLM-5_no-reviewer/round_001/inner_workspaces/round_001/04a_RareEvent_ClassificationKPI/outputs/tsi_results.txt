Temporal Stability Index (TSI) Results
==================================================

TSI Formula:
  TSI = max(0, min(1, 1 - σ_d / (σ_x + ε)))

Where:
  x = 1-D series of model outputs
  d = first differences of x
  σ_x = population std of x (ddof=0)
  σ_d = population std of d (ddof=0)
  ε = 1e-12

Results:
  Number of samples: 5000

  σ_d: 0.079956
