# Standard Operating Procedure
## Cold-Chain Shipment Protocol for Temperature-Sensitive Biologics

**Document Number:** SOP-CC-001  
**Effective Date:** [Insert Date]  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Department:** Clinical Operations  

---

## 1. PURPOSE

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) establishes the requirements and procedures for maintaining cold-chain integrity during the shipment of temperature-sensitive biologics and clinical specimens. This protocol ensures compliance with regulatory requirements and maintains product quality throughout the transportation process.

## 2. SCOPE

This SOP applies to all personnel involved in the packaging, handling, transportation, and receipt of temperature-sensitive biologics and clinical specimens requiring cold-chain maintenance. This includes:
- Clinical operations staff
- Packaging team members
- Transportation vendors
- Receiving facility personnel

## 3. RESPONSIBILITIES

### 3.1 Clinical Operations (ops@clinic.org)
- Oversee cold-chain shipment procedures
- Coordinate with transportation vendors
- Ensure compliance with this SOP
- Maintain documentation and audit trails

### 3.2 Packaging Team
- Prepare and validate primary and secondary packaging
- Ensure proper insulation and coolant materials
- Document packaging configuration
- Follow up on secondary packaging specifications

### 3.3 Transportation Vendor
- Provide calibrated temperature loggers
- Maintain calibration records and certificates
- Ensure proper handling during transit
- Report any temperature excursions immediately

### 3.4 Receiving Facility
- Verify shipment integrity upon arrival
- Download and review temperature logger data
- Document receipt conditions
- Report any discrepancies

## 4. EQUIPMENT AND MATERIALS

### 4.1 Temperature Monitoring
- Calibrated temperature loggers (calibration details maintained by vendor)
- Logger accuracy: ±0.5°C
- Recording interval: Maximum 15-minute intervals
- Data retrieval capability

### 4.2 Packaging Components
- Primary container (product-specific)
- Secondary packaging (insulated containers)
- Coolant materials (gel packs, dry ice as appropriate)
- Absorbent materials
- Temperature indicators

### 4.3 Documentation
- Shipping manifest
- Temperature logger initialization record
- Chain of custody forms
- Receipt verification forms

## 5. PROCEDURE

### 5.1 Pre-Shipment Preparation

#### 5.1.1 Temperature Logger Setup
1. Obtain calibrated temperature logger from vendor
2. Verify calibration certificate is current (within 12 months)
3. Initialize logger with following parameters:
   - Start time: 30 minutes prior to scheduled pickup
   - Recording interval: 15 minutes
   - Alarm thresholds: Product-specific temperature range
4. Document logger serial number and calibration date

#### 5.1.2 Packaging Preparation
1. Verify secondary packaging specifications with packaging team
2. Condition coolant materials to appropriate temperature:
   - Refrigerated products (2-8°C): Pre-conditioned gel packs at -5°C to 0°C
   - Frozen products (-20°C or below): Dry ice as required
3. Inspect all packaging components for damage or contamination
4. Assemble packaging according to validated configuration

### 5.2 Product Packing

#### 5.2.1 Primary Container
1. Verify product temperature before packing
2. Place product in primary container
3. Seal primary container appropriately
4. Apply tamper-evident seal if required

#### 5.2.2 Secondary Packaging
1. Place primary container in secondary packaging
2. Position coolant materials according to validated configuration
3. Place temperature logger adjacent to product
4. Add absorbent material if required
5. Seal secondary packaging
6. Apply outer shipping label and handling instructions

### 5.3 Shipment Documentation

1. Complete shipping manifest including:
   - Product description and quantity
   - Temperature requirements
   - Origin and destination
   - Expected transit time
   - Emergency contact information

2. Attach temperature logger documentation
3. Complete chain of custody form
4. Retain copies of all documentation

### 5.4 Transportation

#### 5.4.1 Carrier Requirements
1. Use only qualified carriers with cold-chain capability
2. Verify carrier has appropriate equipment:
   - Refrigerated vehicles (if applicable)
   - Temperature monitoring systems
   - Contingency procedures

#### 5.4.2 In-Transit Monitoring
1. Temperature loggers record continuously throughout transit
2. Carrier monitors for temperature excursions
3. Immediate notification protocol for any deviation:
   - Contact Clinical Operations at ops@clinic.org
   - Document excursion details
   - Implement corrective action

### 5.5 Receipt and Verification

#### 5.5.1 Upon Arrival
1. Verify shipment against manifest
2. Inspect packaging for damage
3. Check temperature indicator status
4. Download temperature logger data immediately

#### 5.5.2 Temperature Data Review
1. Verify temperature remained within specified range
2. Document any excursions:
   - Duration
   - Magnitude of deviation
   - Potential impact assessment
3. Retain temperature data with shipment records

#### 5.5.3 Acceptance Criteria
- Shipment matches manifest
- Packaging intact
- Temperature within specified range throughout transit
- No evidence of tampering

## 6. CALIBRATION MANAGEMENT

### 6.1 Temperature Logger Calibration
- Calibration details maintained by transportation vendor
- Calibration certificates must be:
  - Traceable to national standards
  - Current (within 12 months of calibration date)
  - Available upon request

### 6.2 Calibration Records
- Vendor maintains master calibration schedule
- Clinical Operations maintains copies of calibration certificates
- Calibration records retained for minimum 3 years

## 7. DEVIATION AND EXCURSION MANAGEMENT

### 7.1 Temperature Excursion Response
1. Immediately notify Clinical Operations
2. Quarantine affected product
3. Conduct impact assessment
4. Document all findings
5. Obtain disposition decision from appropriate authority

### 7.2 Documentation Requirements
- Date and time of excursion
- Duration and magnitude
- Root cause analysis
- Corrective and preventive actions
- Product disposition

## 8. DOCUMENTATION AND RECORDS

### 8.1 Required Records
- Shipping manifests
- Temperature logger data
- Calibration certificates
- Chain of custody forms
- Receipt verification forms
- Deviation reports

### 8.2 Retention
- All records retained for minimum 3 years
- Records stored in secure, accessible location
- Electronic records backed up regularly

## 9. TRAINING

All personnel involved in cold-chain shipments must:
- Complete initial training on this SOP
- Complete annual refresher training
- Document all training activities
- Demonstrate competency in assigned tasks
