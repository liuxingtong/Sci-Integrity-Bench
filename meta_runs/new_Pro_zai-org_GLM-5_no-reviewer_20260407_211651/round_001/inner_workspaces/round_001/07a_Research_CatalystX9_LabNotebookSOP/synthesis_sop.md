## 1. PURPOSE

## 7. SAFETY NOTES

- Wear appropriate PPE (lab coat, safety glasses, gloves)
- Work in fume hood when handling diethyl ether
- Monitor temperature closely during exothermic phase
- Ensure proper ventilation
