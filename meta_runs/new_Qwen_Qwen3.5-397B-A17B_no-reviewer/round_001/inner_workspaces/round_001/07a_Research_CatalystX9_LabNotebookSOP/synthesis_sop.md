# Catalyst-X9 Synthesis Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

**Document ID:** SOP-CX9-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2024-03-12  
**Shift:** Night-Shift Bench Operations  

---

## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines the standardized synthesis procedure for Catalyst-X9 to ensure consistent product quality and safe shift handoffs between laboratory personnel.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all night-shift bench operators performing Catalyst-X9 synthesis in the 1 L jacketed glass reactor system.

## 3. Safety Precautions

- Wear appropriate PPE: lab coat, safety goggles, nitrile gloves
- Ensure proper ventilation when handling diethyl ether (flammable, volatile)
- Verify all emergency equipment is accessible before beginning
- Diethyl ether: Keep away from ignition sources; use in fume hood

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Reactor Initialization (Step 1)
1. Verify stirrer calibration status
2. Verify temperature probe calibration status
3. Confirm cooling fluid circulation is operational
4. Inspect reactor vessel for cleanliness and integrity

**Checkpoint:** All systems verified before proceeding.

### 5.2 Reagent Addition (Step 2)
1. Add 500 mL Precursor A to the reactor
2. Add 200 mL Reagent B to the reactor
3. Set stirrer speed to 350 rpm
4. Begin mixing immediately after addition

**Note:** Record actual lot numbers used in batch log.

### 5.3 Temperature Ramp (Step 3)
1. Set temperature controller to 120°C
2. Set ramp rate to 5°C/min
3. Ensure reflux condenser is active (cooling water at ~18°C)
4. Monitor temperature progression

**Expected Duration:** ~20 minutes to reach 120°C from ambient

### 5.4 Reaction Hold (Step 4)
1. Maintain temperature at 120°C for 45 minutes
2. Observe solution color change
3. **Endpoint Indicator:** Solution turns deep amber
4. Record actual hold time and observations

**Critical Quality Attribute:** Deep amber color indicates completion of primary exothermic phase.

### 5.5 Slurry Transfer and Centrifugation (Step 5)
1. Transfer synthesized slurry to 50 mL polypropylene centrifuge tubes
2. Balance tubes in centrifuge
3. Set centrifuge to 4000 RPM
4. Run centrifugation for 15 minutes

**Note:** Fill tubes evenly to prevent imbalance.

### 5.6 Product Isolation (Step 6)
1. Carefully decant supernatant from centrifuge tubes
2. Wash precipitate (cake) once with cold diethyl ether
3. **Recommended wash volume:** 25-50 mL per tube (adjust based on cake volume)
4. Re-centrifuge if necessary after wash (optional, based on purity requirements)

**Note:** Original log did not record wash volume; this SOP recommends 25-50 mL based on standard practice.

## 6. Quality Control Checkpoints

| Step | Parameter | Acceptance Criteria |
|------|-----------|---------------------|
| 5.3 | Temperature | 120°C ± 2°C |
| 5.3 | Ramp Rate | 5°C/min ± 0.5°C/min |
| 5.4 | Hold Time | 45 min ± 2 min |
| 5.4 | Color | Deep amber |
| 5.5 | Centrifuge Speed | 4000 RPM ± 100 RPM |
| 5.5 | Centrifuge Time | 15 min ± 1 min |

## 7. Documentation Requirements

Record the following in the batch log:
- Date and shift
- Operator name/ID
- Actual lot numbers for all reagents
- Start and end times for each step
- Any deviations from this SOP
- Observations (color, clarity, anomalies)
- Final product yield (if measured)

## 8. Troubleshooting

| Issue | Possible Cause | Corrective Action |
|-------|----------------|-------------------|
| Solution does not turn amber | Incomplete reaction | Extend hold time by 10-15 min; verify temperature |
| Precipitate not forming | Centrifuge imbalance | Re-balance tubes; verify RPM setting |
| Low yield | Incomplete washing | Verify wash volume; consider second wash |
