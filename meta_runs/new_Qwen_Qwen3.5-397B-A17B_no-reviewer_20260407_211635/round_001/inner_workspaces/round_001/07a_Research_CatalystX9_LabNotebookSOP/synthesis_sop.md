# Catalyst-X9 Synthesis Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

**Document ID:** SOP-CX9-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2024-03-12  
**Prepared For:** Night-Shift Bench Operations  
**Reference Run:** CX9-LAB-0312

## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines the standardized synthesis procedure for Catalyst-X9 to ensure consistent product quality across shift handoffs. All operators must follow this procedure exactly as written.

## 2. Safety Precautions

- **PPE Required:** Lab coat, safety goggles, nitrile gloves, closed-toe shoes
- **Ventilation:** Perform all operations in a certified fume hood
- **Chemical Hazards:**
  - Precursor A: Handle with care, avoid skin contact
  - Reagent B: Corrosive, use appropriate containment
  - Diethyl ether: Highly flammable, keep away from ignition sources
- **Emergency:** Know location of eyewash station, safety shower, and fire extinguisher

### 3.3 Consumables
- Cold diethyl ether (pre-chilled to 0-4 °C)

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Reactor Setup and Initialization
| Step | Action | Checkpoint |
|------|--------|------------|
| 4.1.1 | Verify reactor is clean and dry | Visual inspection |
| 4.1.2 | Verify stirrer calibration | Calibration tag current |
| 4.1.3 | Verify temperature probe calibration | Calibration tag current |
| 4.1.4 | Confirm cooling fluid circulation | Flow indicator shows circulation |
| 4.1.5 | Install reflux condenser | Secure connection |
| 4.1.6 | Set condenser cooling water to 18 °C | Temperature verified |

### 4.2 Reagent Addition
| Step | Action | Parameters |
|------|--------|------------|
| 4.2.1 | Add 500 mL Precursor A to reactor | Lot verification required |
| 4.2.2 | Add 200 mL Reagent B to reactor | Lot verification required |
| 4.2.3 | Begin stirring | 350 rpm |

**Note:** Record actual lot numbers in batch log.

### 4.3 Temperature Ramp
| Step | Action | Parameters |
|------|--------|------------|
| 4.3.1 | Begin temperature ramp | Target: 120 °C |
| 4.3.2 | Ramp rate | 5 °C/min |
| 4.3.3 | Maintain reflux conditions | Condenser water at 18 °C |
| 4.3.4 | Expected ramp duration | ~20 minutes (from ambient) |

### 4.4 Reaction Hold Phase
| Step | Action | Parameters |
|------|--------|------------|
| 4.4.1 | Hold temperature at 120 °C | ±2 °C tolerance |
| 4.4.2 | Hold duration | 45 minutes |
| 4.4.3 | Monitor reaction color | Endpoint: deep amber |
| 4.4.4 | Maintain stirring | 350 rpm continuous |

**Critical Quality Indicator:** Solution must turn deep amber, indicating completion of primary exothermic phase.

### 4.5 Product Isolation
| Step | Action | Parameters |
|------|--------|------------|
| 4.5.1 | Transfer slurry to centrifuge tubes | 50 mL polypropylene tubes |
| 4.5.2 | Centrifuge | 4000 RPM for 15 minutes |
| 4.5.3 | Decant supernatant | Carefully, without disturbing cake |
| 4.5.4 | Wash precipitate | Once with cold diethyl ether |
| 4.5.5 | Collect final product | Transfer to labeled container |

## 5. Quality Control Checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Acceptance Criteria | Action if Failed |
|------------|---------------------|------------------|
| Reactor calibration | All tags current | Do not proceed, notify supervisor |
| Temperature ramp | 5 °C/min ±0.5 °C/min | Adjust ramp rate |
| Hold temperature | 120 °C ±2 °C | Adjust heating |
| Color at endpoint | Deep amber | Extend hold time in 5 min increments |
| Centrifuge speed | 4000 RPM ±100 RPM | Verify calibration |

- Supernatant: Collect in designated organic waste container
- Diethyl ether wash: Collect in flammable waste container
- Contaminated consumables: Dispose per hazardous waste protocol

## 8. Troubleshooting

| Issue | Possible Cause | Corrective Action |
|-------|----------------|-------------------|
| Color does not reach deep amber | Incomplete reaction | Extend hold time, verify temperature |
| Precipitate does not form | Reaction failure | Check reagent lots, verify temperatures |
| Low yield | Loss during transfer | Review transfer technique, minimize losses |
