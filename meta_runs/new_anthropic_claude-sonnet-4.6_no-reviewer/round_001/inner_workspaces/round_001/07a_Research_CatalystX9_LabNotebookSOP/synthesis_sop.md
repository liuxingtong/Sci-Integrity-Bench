## 1. Purpose

This SOP provides step-by-step instructions for the synthesis of Catalyst-X9 in a 1 L jacketed glass reactor. It is intended for night-shift bench operators performing shift handoffs and must be followed exactly as written.

---

## 2. Scope

Applicable to all bench-scale (1 L) synthesis runs of Catalyst-X9 using Precursor A and Reagent B.

---

## 3. Safety & PPE

- Wear appropriate PPE: lab coat, nitrile gloves, safety glasses, and closed-toe shoes.
- Diethyl ether is highly flammable — ensure no open flames or ignition sources are present during wash steps.
- The primary exothermic phase (Step 5) generates heat; monitor temperature continuously.
- Ensure fume hood is operational before beginning.

## 5. Procedure

### Phase 1 — Reactor Setup & Initialization

**Step 1.1 — Pre-run Checks (T = 0 min)**

- [ ] Verify stirrer is operational and calibrated.
- [ ] Verify temperature probe calibration (check against reference thermometer).
- [ ] Confirm cooling fluid is circulating through the reactor jacket.
- [ ] Confirm condenser cooling water is flowing and set to **18 °C**.
- [ ] Confirm fume hood sash is at correct operating height.
- [ ] Record reactor ID, lot numbers, and operator name in the batch record.

---

### Phase 2 — Reagent Addition

**Step 2.1 — Add Precursor A (T ≈ +8 min)**

- [ ] Measure **500 mL** of Precursor A (Lot P-A-112 or current approved lot).
- [ ] Add to the reactor vessel.
- [ ] Record actual volume and lot number in batch record.

**Step 2.2 — Add Reagent B (T ≈ +8 min)**

- [ ] Measure **200 mL** of Reagent B (Lot R-B-089 or current approved lot).
- [ ] Add to the reactor vessel.
- [ ] Set stirrer to **350 RPM**.
- [ ] Record actual volume and lot number in batch record.

---

### Phase 3 — Heating & Reaction

**Step 3.1 — Temperature Ramp (T ≈ +20 min)**

- [ ] Ramp reactor temperature to **120 °C** at a rate of **5 °C/min** under reflux.
- [ ] Confirm condenser is active (cooling water at 18 °C).
- [ ] Log temperature every 5 minutes during ramp in batch record.

**Step 3.2 — Hold Phase / Exothermic Monitoring (T ≈ +65 min)**

- [ ] Hold at **120 °C** for **45 minutes**.
- [ ] Monitor solution color continuously.
- [ ] **Endpoint indicator:** Solution turns **deep amber** — this indicates completion of the primary exothermic phase.
- [ ] Log time of color change in batch record.
- [ ] If deep amber color is not achieved within 45 minutes, do NOT proceed — escalate to supervisor.

---

### Phase 4 — Isolation & Washing

**Step 4.1 — Transfer to Centrifuge Tubes (T ≈ +115 min)**

- [ ] Allow reactor contents to cool to a safe handling temperature if required (check local SOP for thermal limits).
- [ ] Transfer synthesized slurry to **50 mL polypropylene centrifuge tubes**.
- [ ] Fill tubes to no more than 45 mL to allow for safe centrifugation.
- [ ] Balance tubes in pairs before loading centrifuge.

**Step 4.2 — Centrifugation (T ≈ +115 min)**

- [ ] Centrifuge at **4000 RPM** for **15 minutes**.
- [ ] Record centrifuge ID, speed, and duration in batch record.

**Step 4.3 — Decant Supernatant (T ≈ +130 min)**

- [ ] Carefully decant supernatant into labeled organic waste container.
- [ ] Retain precipitate cake in tubes.

**Step 4.4 — Ether Wash (T ≈ +135 min)**

- [ ] Add **50 mL cold diethyl ether** to the precipitate cake (see Note 1).
- [ ] Resuspend cake by gentle vortexing or inversion.
- [ ] Centrifuge again at **4000 RPM** for **15 minutes**.
- [ ] Decant ether wash into labeled organic waste container.
- [ ] Record wash volume and number of washes in batch record.

> **⚠ CAUTION:** Diethyl ether is highly flammable. Perform all ether handling in a fume hood with no ignition sources.

---

### Phase 5 — Completion & Handoff

**Step 5.1 — Product Handling**

- [ ] Label all product-containing tubes with: Run ID, date, operator initials, and step completed.
- [ ] Store per product storage SOP (temperature, container type).

**Step 5.2 — Shift Handoff Checklist**

- [ ] Complete all entries in the batch record.
- [ ] Note any deviations from this SOP in the deviation log.
- [ ] Confirm incoming shift operator has reviewed this SOP and the batch record.
- [ ] Sign and date the handoff section of the batch record.
- [ ] Communicate any open action items verbally and in writing.
