## 🚨 Quick Safety Reference
- **PPE Required:** Lab coat, safety goggles, nitrile gloves
- **Hazardous Materials:** Diethyl ether (flammable), exothermic reaction at 120°C
- **Emergency:** Know shower/eyewash locations, fire extinguisher

## ⏱️ Step-by-Step Procedure
### Step 1: Setup (Start: T=0 minutes)
1. Initialize reactor in fume hood
2. Verify stirrer (350 RPM capability) and temperature probe
3. Confirm cooling fluid circulation
4. Set up reflux condenser with 18°C cooling water
**✅ Check:** All systems operational before adding reagents

### Step 2: Reagent Addition (T=0-20 minutes)
1. Add 500 mL Precursor A (lot P-A-112) to reactor
2. Add 200 mL Reagent B (lot R-B-089)
3. Begin stirring at 350 RPM
**📝 Record:** Actual volumes, lot numbers, start time

### Step 3: Temperature Ramp (T=20-65 minutes)
1. Ramp temperature to 120°C at 5°C/min
2. Maintain reflux (condenser at 18°C)
3. Monitor for exotherm - temperature may overshoot briefly
**⚠️ Caution:** Reaction becomes exothermic at ~100°C

### Step 4: Reaction Hold (T=65-110 minutes)
1. Hold at 120°C for 45 minutes
2. Monitor color change: clear → deep amber
3. Visual endpoint: uniform deep amber color
**✅ Success Criteria:** Color change complete within 45 min

### Step 5: Workup (T=110-140 minutes)
1. Cool reaction mixture to <50°C
2. Transfer slurry to 50 mL centrifuge tubes (balance tubes)
3. Centrifuge at 4000 RPM for 15 minutes
4. Decant supernatant to waste container
5. Wash precipitate with cold diethyl ether (10 mL per tube)
6. Centrifuge again at 4000 RPM for 5 minutes
7. Decant ether wash
**📝 Record:** Appearance, approximate yield, any issues

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Guide
### Common Issues and Solutions

#### Issue: No color change after 45 minutes at 120°C
**Possible causes:**
1. Temperature probe miscalibration
2. Incorrect reagent lots or degraded materials
3. Insufficient mixing
**Actions:**
1. Verify temperature with independent thermometer
2. Check reagent expiration dates
3. Increase stirring to 400 RPM for 15 minutes
4. If no change after 60 min total, abort and document

#### Issue: Excessive foaming or bumping
**Possible causes:**
1. Too rapid heating
2. Overfilled reactor
**Actions:**
1. Reduce heating rate to 3°C/min
2. Ensure reactor not >70% capacity
3. Consider anti-foaming agent if recurrent issue

#### Issue: Poor precipitation or low yield
**Possible causes:**
1. Incorrect reagent ratios
2. Cooling too rapid before centrifugation
3. Ether wash volume too high
**Actions:**
1. Double-check volume measurements
2. Cool slowly to room temperature before workup
3. Reduce ether wash to 5 mL per tube
