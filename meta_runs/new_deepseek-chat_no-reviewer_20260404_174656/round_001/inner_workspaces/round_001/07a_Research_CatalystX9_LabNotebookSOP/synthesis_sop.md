# Standard Operating Procedure: Synthesis of Catalyst-X9

## 1.0 Purpose
This SOP describes the procedure for synthesizing Catalyst-X9 in a 1 L jacketed glass reactor.

## 2.0 Scope
This procedure applies to laboratory technicians performing Catalyst-X9 synthesis during all shifts.

## 3.0 Safety Precautions
- Wear appropriate PPE: lab coat, safety glasses, nitrile gloves, closed-toe shoes
- Work in a fume hood when handling volatile reagents
- Be aware of exothermic reaction during primary phase
- Have appropriate fire extinguisher and spill kit available
- Know location of emergency shower and eyewash station

## 4.0 Equipment and Materials
### 4.1 Equipment
- 1 L jacketed glass reactor with overhead stirrer
- Temperature controller and probe
- Reflux condenser with cooling water circulation (18 °C)
- Heating mantle or oil bath
- Centrifuge (capable of 4000 RPM)
- 50 mL polypropylene centrifuge tubes
- Analytical balance
- Graduated cylinders (500 mL, 200 mL)
- Vacuum filtration setup (if needed)
- Drying oven

### 4.2 Materials
- Precursor A (lot P-A-112 or current valid lot)
- Reagent B (lot R-B-089 or current valid lot)
- Diethyl ether (cold, for washing)
- Cooling fluid for reactor jacket
- Distilled or deionized water for condenser

## 5.0 Procedure

### 5.1 Pre-Synthesis Setup
1. Verify all equipment is clean and dry.
2. Calibrate temperature probe and stirrer according to manufacturer instructions.
3. Set up reflux condenser with cooling water at 18 °C.
4. Ensure cooling fluid circulation system is operational for reactor jacket.
5. Prepare work area with all necessary materials and equipment.

### 5.2 Reaction Setup
1. Add 500 mL of Precursor A to the 1 L jacketed glass reactor.
2. Add 200 mL of Reagent B to the reactor.
3. Begin stirring at 350 RPM.

### 5.3 Heating Phase
1. Ramp temperature to 120 °C at a rate of 5 °C/min.
2. Maintain reflux conditions throughout heating.
3. Monitor reaction mixture for color change.

### 5.4 Reaction Hold
1. Hold at 120 °C for 45 minutes.
2. Observe solution color change to deep amber, indicating completion of primary exothermic phase.
3. Monitor temperature to ensure stability.

### 5.5 Product Isolation
1. Transfer the synthesized slurry to 50 mL polypropylene centrifuge tubes.
2. Balance tubes appropriately for centrifugation.
3. Centrifuge at 4000 RPM for 15 minutes.
4. Carefully decant supernatant.

### 5.6 Washing and Drying
1. Wash the precipitate cake with cold diethyl ether.
   - Recommended volume: 20 mL per tube (adjust based on cake size)
2. Repeat centrifugation at 4000 RPM for 5 minutes.
3. Decant ether wash.
4. Transfer product to appropriate container for drying.
5. Dry product under vacuum or in drying oven at 40-50 °C until constant weight is achieved.

### 5.7 Cleanup
1. Clean all glassware and equipment according to laboratory cleaning procedures.
2. Properly dispose of waste according to laboratory waste management protocols.

## 6.0 Quality Control
- Record lot numbers of all materials used
- Document reaction times, temperatures, and observations
- Weigh final product and calculate yield
- Perform quality tests as specified in QC protocol CX9-QC-001

## 7.0 Troubleshooting
| Issue | Possible Cause | Solution |
|-------|---------------|----------|
| No color change after 45 min | Incorrect temperature | Verify temperature probe calibration |
| Excessive foaming | Rapid heating | Reduce heating rate to 3 °C/min |
| Poor precipitation | Incorrect reagent ratio | Verify volumes of Precursor A and Reagent B |
| Low yield | Incomplete reaction | Extend hold time by 15 minutes |
