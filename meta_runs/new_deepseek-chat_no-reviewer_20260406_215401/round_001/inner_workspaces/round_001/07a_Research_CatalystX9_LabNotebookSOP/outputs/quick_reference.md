# Catalyst-X9 Synthesis: Quick Reference

## Key Steps
1. **Setup:** Reactor + condenser + cooling (18°C)
2. **Charge:** 500 mL Precursor A + 200 mL Reagent B
3. **Stir:** 350 RPM
4. **Heat:** Ramp to 120 °C at 5 °C/min
5. **Hold:** 45 minutes until deep amber color
6. **Centrifuge:** 4000 RPM for 15 minutes
7. **Wash:** Cold diethyl ether
8. **Dry:** Transfer to vial for drying

## Critical Parameters
- Stirring Speed: 350 RPM
- Temperature: 120 °C
- Ramp Rate: 5 °C/min
- Hold Time: 45 minutes

## Safety Notes
- Wear PPE: glasses, gloves, lab coat
- Use fume hood for ether handling
- Monitor exotherm during heating
