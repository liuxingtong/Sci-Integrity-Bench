# Standard Operating Procedure: Catalyst-X9 Synthesis

## 1.0 Purpose
This SOP describes the synthesis procedure for Catalyst-X9 from Precursor A and Reagent B.

## 2.0 Scope
Applies to all laboratory personnel performing Catalyst-X9 synthesis during night shifts.

## 3.0 Responsibilities
- Bench chemist: Execute procedure as written
- Shift supervisor: Verify critical steps and sign-off

## 4.0 Materials and Equipment
### 4.1 Chemicals
- Precursor A: 500 mL (Lot: P-A-112)
- Reagent B: 200 mL (Lot: R-B-089)
- Diethyl ether (cold, for washing)

### 4.2 Equipment
- 1 L jacketed glass reactor
- Overhead stirrer with calibrated probe
- Reflux condenser with cooling water (18°C)
- Temperature controller
- 50 mL polypropylene centrifuge tubes
- Centrifuge capable of 4000 RPM
- Fume hood for ether handling

## 5.0 Safety Precautions
- Wear appropriate PPE: lab coat, safety glasses, nitrile gloves
- Work in fume hood when handling diethyl ether
- Be aware of exothermic reaction during heating phase
- Have fire extinguisher and spill kit readily available

## 6.0 Procedure
### 6.1 Preparation
1. Verify all equipment is clean and calibrated.
2. Confirm cooling fluid circulation is operational.
3. Set up reactor in fume hood with proper connections.

### 6.2 Synthesis Steps
1. **14:00** — Initialized the reactor, verified stirrer and temperature probe calibration, and confirmed cooling fluid circulation.
2. **14:08** — Added 500 mL Precursor A (lot P-A-112), then 200 mL Reagent B (lot R-B-089). Mixed at 350 rpm.
3. **14:20** — Ramped temperature to 120 °C at 5 °C/min under reflux (condenser water 18 °C).
4. **15:05** — Held at 120 °C for 45 minutes until the solution turned deep amber, indicating completion of the primary exothermic phase.
5. **16:55** — Decanted supernatant; cake washed once with cold diethyl ether (volume not recorded in this excerpt).
6. Transfer synthesized slurry to 50 mL polypropylene centrifuge tubes.
7. Centrifuge at 4000 RPM for 15 minutes.
8. Decant supernatant carefully.
9. Wash precipitate once with cold diethyl ether (volume: 50 mL per tube).
10. Collect product for drying and characterization.

### 6.3 Critical Parameters
- **Stirring Speed**: 350 RPM
- **Temperature**: 120 °C
- **Ramp Rate**: 5 °C/min
- **Hold Time**: 45 minutes

## 7.0 Quality Control
- Visual check: Solution should turn deep amber after 45 minutes at 120°C
- Product yield should be recorded
- Sample should be sent for XRD and BET analysis

## 8.0 Troubleshooting
| Issue | Possible Cause | Solution |
|-------|----------------|----------|
| No color change | Temperature too low | Verify temperature probe calibration |
| Excessive foaming | Stirring too fast | Reduce to 300 RPM |
| Poor precipitation | Cooling too slow | Ensure condenser water at 18°C |
