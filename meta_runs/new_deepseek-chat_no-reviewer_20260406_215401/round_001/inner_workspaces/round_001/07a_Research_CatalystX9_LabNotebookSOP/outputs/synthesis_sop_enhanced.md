# Standard Operating Procedure: Catalyst-X9 Synthesis
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective Date:** 2024-03-15 | **SOP ID:** CX9-SOP-001

---

## 1.0 PURPOSE AND SCOPE
### 1.1 Purpose
This document provides step-by-step instructions for synthesizing Catalyst-X9 from Precursor A and Reagent B. The procedure is optimized for reproducibility and safety during night-shift operations.

### 1.2 Scope
This SOP applies to all trained laboratory personnel authorized to perform chemical synthesis in the catalyst development laboratory.

## 2.0 RESPONSIBILITIES
- **Bench Chemist:** Execute the procedure exactly as written, document any deviations, and record all observations.
- **Shift Supervisor:** Review critical steps, verify calculations, and provide final sign-off before product release.
- **Quality Assurance:** Periodically audit procedure compliance and review batch records.

## 4.0 SAFETY AND PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE)
### 4.1 Hazard Assessment
- **Exothermic reaction:** Temperature control critical during heating phase
- **Flammable solvents:** Diethyl ether requires fume hood use
- **Chemical exposure:** Wear appropriate PPE at all times

### 4.2 Required PPE
- Lab coat (fire-resistant recommended)
- Safety glasses with side shields or goggles
- Nitrile gloves (double-glove for ether handling)
- Closed-toe shoes

## 5.0 PROCEDURE
### 5.1 Pre-Start Checklist
- [ ] Fume hood operational and certified
- [ ] All equipment clean and calibrated
- [ ] Cooling water circulation confirmed (18°C)
- [ ] Fire extinguisher and spill kit accessible
- [ ] Batch record form prepared

### 5.2 Synthesis Protocol
**Step 1: Reactor Setup**
1. Assemble the 1 L jacketed glass reactor with overhead stirrer, temperature probe, and reflux condenser.
2. Verify all connections are secure and leak-free.
3. Start cooling water circulation through condenser (set to 18°C).
4. Begin nitrogen purge through reactor headspace (optional but recommended).

**Step 2: Charging Reactants**
1. Add 500 mL of Precursor A (Lot: P-A-112) to reactor.
2. Add 200 mL of Reagent B (Lot: R-B-089) to reactor.
3. Start stirring at 350 RPM.
4. Record initial observations (color, homogeneity).

**Step 3: Heating Phase**
1. Set temperature controller to ramp to 120 °C at 5 °C/min.
2. Monitor temperature closely during ramp-up.
3. Once at target temperature, start timer for hold period.

**Step 4: Reaction Monitoring**
1. Maintain temperature at 120 °C for 45 minutes.
2. Observe color change: solution should turn deep amber within 30-45 minutes.
3. Record time when color change is complete.

**Step 5: Product Isolation**
1. Turn off heating and allow reaction mixture to cool to <50°C.
2. Transfer slurry to pre-labeled 50 mL polypropylene centrifuge tubes.
3. Centrifuge at 4000 RPM for 15 minutes.
4. Carefully decant supernatant into appropriate waste container.

**Step 6: Washing and Final Processing**
1. Wash precipitate with cold diethyl ether (50 mL per tube).
2. Resuspend by vortexing or gentle shaking.
3. Repeat centrifugation at 4000 RPM for 5 minutes.
4. Decant wash solvent.
5. Transfer wet product to labeled glass vial for drying.

## 6.0 CRITICAL PROCESS PARAMETERS
| Parameter | Target Value | Acceptable Range | Monitoring Frequency |
|-----------|--------------|------------------|----------------------|
| Stirring Speed | 350 RPM | 300-400 RPM | Continuous |
| Reaction Temperature | 120 °C | 118-122°C | Every 5 minutes |
| Ramp Rate | 5 °C/min | 4-6°C/min | During ramp only |
| Hold Time | 45 minutes | 40-50 minutes | Timer |
| Condenser Water Temp | 18°C | 15-20°C | Start and midpoint |

## 7.0 QUALITY CONTROL CHECKS
### 7.1 In-Process Controls
- **Visual inspection:** Solution must turn deep amber within hold period
- **Temperature verification:** Independent thermometer reading within ±2°C of controller
- **Stirring verification:** Visual confirmation of adequate mixing

### 7.2 Final Product Specifications
- **Appearance:** Fine amber powder
- **Yield:** Target 65-75% of theoretical
- **Purity:** ≥95% by XRD phase analysis
- **Surface area:** 150-200 m²/g by BET

## 8.0 TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE
| Symptom | Possible Cause | Corrective Action |
|---------|----------------|-------------------|
| No color change after 45 min | Temperature too low | Verify probe calibration; increase setpoint 2°C |
| Excessive foaming | Stirring too vigorous | Reduce speed to 300 RPM |
| Poor precipitation | Cooling rate too slow | Check condenser water flow; ensure 18°C |
| Low yield | Incomplete reaction or loss during transfer | Extend hold time 10 min; improve transfer technique |
| Product discoloration | Overheating or contamination | Verify temperature control; use fresh reagents |
