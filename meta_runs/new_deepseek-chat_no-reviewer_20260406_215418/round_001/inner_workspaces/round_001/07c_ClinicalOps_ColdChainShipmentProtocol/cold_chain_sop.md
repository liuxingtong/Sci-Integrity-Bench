# Standard Operating Procedure: Cold-Chain Shipment of Temperature-Sensitive Biologics

## 1.0 Purpose
To establish standardized procedures for the shipment of temperature-sensitive biologics, ensuring maintenance of the cold chain from origin to destination, in compliance with regulatory requirements.

## 2.0 Scope
This SOP applies to all personnel involved in the packaging, labeling, documentation, loading, transportation, and receipt of temperature-sensitive biologics shipped via the new biologics route.

## 3.0 Definitions
- **Cold Chain**: The temperature-controlled supply chain for products requiring specific temperature ranges.
- **Biologics**: Biological products derived from living organisms.
- **Data Logger**: Electronic device that records temperature over time.
- **Secondary Packaging**: Outer packaging that provides additional protection and insulation.

## 4.0 Responsibilities
- **Clinical Operations**: Overall responsibility for SOP compliance.
- **Packaging Team**: Responsible for proper secondary packaging and preparation.
- **Logistics Team**: Responsible for transportation and monitoring.
- **Vendor**: Provides calibration details for data loggers.

## 5.0 Procedure

### 5.1 Pre-Shipment Preparation
1. Verify product storage conditions and required temperature range.
2. Ensure adequate quantity of validated cold-chain packaging materials.
3. Confirm data loggers are calibrated (calibration details maintained by vendor).
4. Document pre-shipment checks on the Cold Chain Shipment Checklist.

### 5.2 Packaging
1. Place product in primary container.
2. Add appropriate coolant packs (frozen gel packs, dry ice, etc.) as per validated protocol.
3. Install activated data logger inside secondary packaging.
4. Seal secondary packaging according to manufacturer's instructions.
5. Apply temperature-sensitive labels (e.g., "Keep Refrigerated", "Do Not Freeze").

### 5.3 Documentation
1. Complete shipping manifest with product details, temperature requirements, and emergency contact information.
2. Include data logger retrieval instructions for recipient.
3. Attach required regulatory documents.

### 5.4 Loading and Transportation
1. Load packaged shipments into pre-cooled trucks equipped with temperature monitoring systems.
2. Verify truck temperature is within required range before loading.
3. Secure packages to prevent movement during transit.
4. Activate truck data loggers and confirm they are recording.

### 5.5 Monitoring and Tracking
1. Monitor temperature data remotely if available.
2. Track shipment location throughout transit.
3. Implement contingency plan if temperature excursion occurs.

### 5.6 Receipt and Verification
1. Recipient must inspect package immediately upon arrival.
2. Verify temperature data from data loggers.
3. Document any temperature excursions on Temperature Excursion Report form.
4. Store product at appropriate conditions immediately.

## 6.0 Temperature Excursion Management
1. If temperature excursion occurs, quarantine product.
2. Notify Quality Assurance and Clinical Operations immediately.
3. Conduct impact assessment per established procedures.
4. Document all actions taken.

## 7.0 Training
All personnel involved in cold-chain shipments must complete training on this SOP before performing related activities.
