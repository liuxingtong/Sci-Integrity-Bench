# Standard Operating Procedure: Catalyst-X9 Synthesis

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This SOP details the synthesis procedure for Catalyst-X9, designed for night-shift bench use. It is derived from Run ID: CX9-LAB-0312.

## 2. Equipment and Materials
- **Reactor:** 1 L jacketed glass reactor
- **Temperature Control:** Heating/cooling circulator, temperature probe
- **Agitation:** Overhead stirrer
- **Condenser:** Reflux condenser (cooling water at 18 °C)
- **Centrifuge:** Capable of 4000 RPM, with 50 mL polypropylene centrifuge tubes
- **Reagents:**
  - Precursor A (500 mL)
  - Reagent B (200 mL)
  - Cold diethyl ether (for washing)

## 3. Safety Precautions
- Ensure all cooling fluid circulation is active before heating.
- Handle hot glassware and reactor contents with appropriate thermal PPE.
- Diethyl ether is highly flammable; handle only in a well-ventilated fume hood away from ignition sources.

## 4. Procedure

### Phase 1: Reactor Initialization and Charging
1. **Initialize Reactor:** Verify stirrer calibration, temperature probe calibration, and confirm cooling fluid circulation.
2. **Charge Precursor A:** Add 500 mL of Precursor A to the 1 L jacketed glass reactor.
3. **Charge Reagent B:** Add 200 mL of Reagent B to the reactor.
4. **Agitation:** Start the overhead stirrer and set the speed to **350 rpm**.

### Phase 2: Reaction and Heating
5. **Reflux Setup:** Ensure condenser water is flowing at 18 °C.
6. **Temperature Ramp:** Initiate heating to ramp the reactor temperature to **120 °C** at a rate of **5 °C/min**.
7. **Hold Phase:** Once the temperature reaches 120 °C, hold the temperature for **45 minutes**.
   - *Observation:* The solution should turn deep amber, indicating the completion of the primary exothermic phase.

### Phase 3: Isolation and Washing
8. **Transfer:** Transfer the synthesized slurry directly into 50 mL polypropylene centrifuge tubes.
9. **Centrifugation:** Spin the tubes at **4000 RPM for 15 minutes** to isolate the precipitate.
10. **Decantation:** Carefully decant and discard the supernatant.
11. **Washing:** Wash the resulting filter cake once with cold diethyl ether.
