# Standard Operating Procedure: Cold-Chain Shipment for Biologics

## 1. Purpose
To establish a standard operating procedure for the cold-chain shipment of biologics along the new route.

## 2. Scope
This SOP applies to all clinical operations personnel involved in the transportation and handling of biologics requiring cold-chain maintenance.

## 3. Responsibilities
- **Clinical Operations Team:** Ensure adherence to this SOP during shipment.
- **Vendor:** Maintain and provide calibration details for temperature loggers used in transport trucks.
- **Packaging Team:** Manage and follow up on secondary packaging requirements.

## 4. Procedure
### 4.1. Temperature Monitoring
- All transport trucks must be equipped with temperature loggers.
- Calibration details for these loggers are maintained by the vendor and must be accessible upon request.

### 4.2. Packaging
- Primary packaging of biologics must be completed prior to shipment.
- The Packaging Team is responsible for following up on and ensuring the integrity of secondary packaging.

## 5. References
- Email directive from ops@clinic.org regarding new biologics route.
