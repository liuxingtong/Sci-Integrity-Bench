Huangtupo radiocarbon bundle — data notes
=========================================

Column semantics
----------------
- f14_residual_ratio: fraction of modern ¹⁴C (1.0 ≡ 100 pMC on the lab’s stated primary standard scale).
- sigma_f14_absolute: laboratory 1σ uncertainty on that fraction (absolute, same scale as the ratio).
- stratigraphic_unit: excavation label for provenience (provisional; not a substitute for full Harris matrix).
- material / laboratory_batch / notes: as provided by the lab or field team.

F14 to conventional radiocarbon years BP
----------------------------------------
One common mapping for exercises is Libby’s mean life form: age_BP = -8033 * ln(f14_residual_ratio). Clip F14 into (0, 1) only to avoid numerical failure, and note any clips in your workflow.
